How to play

Three ways out: get the front door open, cut the chain on the back-yard gate, or get Tim’s car running in the garage. He hunts by sound: running, slamming doors, dropped pans and creaky boards all bring him to you. If he sees you, break line of sight and hide. Vault couches, the kitchen island, fallen branches and the stair rail to lose him; squeeze through holes he can’t fit through. If he checks out a noise and finds nothing, he leaves a mousetrap behind. You have five days.
